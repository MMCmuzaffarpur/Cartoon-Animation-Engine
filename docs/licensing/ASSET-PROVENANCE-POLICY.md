# Asset Provenance Policy

Each asset revision requires immutable content hash, source, creator/importer, acquisition date, license/terms, attribution text, redistribution rights, dependency/derivative relation, and compatibility metadata. Generated assets additionally require GenerationProvenance: adapter/model/tool and version, weights identity/license, request hash, seed, inputs, settings, environment and output hashes.

Asset variants and Character Variants preserve base asset references and license obligations. Exports collect notices/attribution for included assets. Assets with missing rights, source, or redistribution information remain quarantined and cannot enter production render/export workflows.
